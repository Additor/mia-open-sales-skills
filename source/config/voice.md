# Voice

How one person writes to buyers, so drafts read like them on a good day.

## Register

Three or four specific traits, each with a short real example.

## Writing patterns

Greeting, sign-off, typical length, bullets or prose, how an ask is phrased, subject lines. Note whether the email tool adds a signature, so drafts do not add another.

## Situation samples

One trimmed sample for each: first follow-up, recap after a demo, check-in on a quiet deal, pricing, scheduling, quick reply. Skip any situation with no real sample.

## Signature phrases

Five to eight lines, word for word.

## Reply signals

Patterns that set replied-to emails apart from ignored ones. Include a pattern only if both groups contain 8 or more emails, and show the totals.

## Avoid

Expressions and behaviors this person would never use.
