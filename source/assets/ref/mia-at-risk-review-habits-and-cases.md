# At-risk review: habits and special situations

Read this for Part 2 and whenever a source or file is missing.

## Default habits
Used when no pack file sets a standard; say that they were used. Follow up within 1 working day of each buyer call. Every open deal has a valid next step (scoring-axes `## Definitions`). A two-way touch with the buyer at least every 14 days. Two or more buyer contacts before a proposal or price. Economic Buyer identified before a proposal. Close date backed by recent activity, and a stage that matches the work. CRM updated within 1 working day of a call.

## Part 2: check the habits
Choose 3 to 5 habits and measure each as a count over in-scope deals from the last 30 to 90 days ("recap sent within a day after 4 of 13 demos"). Sources in order:
1. working-style `## Moves from my best deals` and `## Recurring slips`; winning-plays `## Habits that set our best deals apart` and `## Moves that get skipped`.
2. team-standards `## Every deal gets` and `## Working rules`.
3. Without files, compare up to 20 recent wins with open deals and use habits most wins show and open deals lack (a second contact by a certain stage, a recap after demos, a booked next meeting, a business case before price), marked "Inferred from wins". With fewer than 5 wins, use default habits.

Report only habits the connected data can measure and omit the rest; name the omitted ones in the Habits note (for example call quality without a recording tool). A habit measured only from logged email counts is omitted when fewer than 10 logged outbound emails exist in the window. In team view show each habit per rep. Where one pattern of work stands out, state it once in the Habits note (schema), as what the work looks like with counts on both sides. Describe work, never people.

## Special situations
- No CRM and no CSV: begin by stating that the review requires deals, and request either a CRM connection or a CSV listing open deals (next step, last activity, close date, amount, stage, owner). Where calls and email are linked, make a single offer right away, for instance checking, across the past 30 days of calls, whether promised follow-ups were sent. Do not invent a deal list.
- CSV: read as the CRM following `assets/ref/shared-csv-as-crm.md`; note unchecked gaps for missing columns in `howBuilt`.
- No call recordings: skip call-based promises and "Call not logged" (use calendar meetings to test CRM updates) and say so.
- No email: promises cannot be verified, so label them "unconfirmed" and tally them on their own; assess quiet days using chat, the calendar and the CRM alone.
- Source fails mid-run: retry once, continue, mark totals partial (for example, email stopped at 18 threads).
- Under 5 open deals in the view's scope: run the same template; the lists are short and the shared rule empties what the data cannot fill. No amounts: count deals.
- Pack files missing or seed (shared definitions): use default habits and say so in `measuredAgainst`.
